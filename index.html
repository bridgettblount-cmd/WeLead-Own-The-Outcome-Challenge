The Challenge

Turn leadership learning into action.

Choose an important business metric. Understand the teammate behaviors that influence it. Take action through coaching, accountability and partnership. Then tell us what happened.

There’s no required format. Use the challenge toolkit, your own tools, or whatever works best for your team. What matters is doing the work and connecting leadership behaviors to business outcomes.
The Leadership Mindset Shift

Own more than the coaching conversation.

FROM

“Leadership owns the metrics. I coach the people.”

TO

“As leaders, we own the metrics because we own the behaviors that drive them.”

Explore the Challenge

A 7 phase roadmap to guide the work.

Use as much or as little of the roadmap as you need. Click each phase for a quick overview.

Identify the metrics that matter most, why they matter, how they are calculated, where leaders find them and what healthy performance looks like.

Identify observable teammate behaviors that directly influence each metric. Focus on behaviors leaders can see, coach and reinforce.

Look for behaviors that influence multiple metrics. These become powerful coaching priorities because one behavior can move several outcomes.

Define what great looks like, common misses, coaching strategies, recognition and the measures that will tell you whether the approach is working.

Agree on what leaders will coach consistently, how often progress will be reviewed, how success will be measured and when to adjust.

Partner across NOE, peer leaders, managers, senior managers, Operations and Learning & Development to validate findings and strengthen the work.

Share what you learned, gather feedback, commit to next actions and turn the work into something your leadership team can use.
